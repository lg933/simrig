## Where things stand

- Full F1 calendar learned via brake-sign-anchored braking points (not just lap familiarity). Reached ~2.5s off real-world lap records; 1:28 at Jeddah in time trial — near real-world pole pace. Elite/110 difficulty, all assists off, manual gearbox.
- 4-month gap (Feb–June 2026) for EPFL BA6 finals — bachelor's completed. Post-break, raw pace held up; ran a structured reactivation (brake-sign recall, one track at a time, benchmarked against the 1:28 Jeddah anchor) rather than relearning from scratch.
- **Current limiter is racecraft, not pace**: overtaking, defending, traffic management, situational reading. This is the development priority, not lap time chasing.
- History of restarting sim racing progress (memorize → reset → repeat) without converting pace into racecraft. Directive going forward: **commit and continue, don't reset.**

## Working principles

- Difficulty calibration: target 0.1–0.3s gap to AI pole, not grid position.
- Setups: one parameter at a time, one symptom at a time. Steam Workshop setups as baseline, not built from scratch.
- Brake bias is already adjusted per circuit as a matter of course.
